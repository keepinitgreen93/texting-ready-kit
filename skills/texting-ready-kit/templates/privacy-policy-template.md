# Privacy Policy Template (Texting Ready)

This template is a starting point, not legal advice. Have an attorney review it for your state and industry.

**Healthcare, HIPAA or other regulated businesses:** check with your attorney before you use this. Do not put private health details in text messages unless your counsel has approved the setup.

## How to fill this in

1. Replace every [BRACKETED] item with your real facts. Use the exact same business name, address, phone and email you will enter when you register for texting.
2. If you do not use a DBA (a "doing business as" name), delete every "doing business as" phrase.
3. Delete any line that starts with "OPTIONAL" if it does not apply to you.
4. Delete this "How to fill this in" part and everything above the line that begins with the word START. Paste only the policy.
5. Do not add language about buying, selling or sharing lists of people. Reviewers reject it.
