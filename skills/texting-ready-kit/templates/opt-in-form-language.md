# Opt-In Form Language (Texting Ready)

This template is a starting point, not legal advice. Have an attorney review it for your state and industry.

Reviewers check your form with their own eyes. The words below sit right next to your phone number field. They must match your campaign description, your Privacy Policy and your Terms.

## The rules for the checkbox

1. **Unchecked by default.** The person must tick the box themselves.
2. **Optional.** The form must submit without the box ticked, even if the phone number is required.
3. **Separate.** Do not tie text consent to "I agree to the Terms" or to email or phone call consent. It must name text messages.
4. **Marketing and non-marketing are separate boxes** when you send both (our default campaign type, Low Volume Mixed, does). A person can pick one, both or neither.
5. **Say who and what.** Name your business and the kinds of texts, using the same words as your campaign description.
6. **Include the basics next to the box:** message frequency, "message and data rates may apply," how to STOP, how to get HELP, and links to your Privacy Policy and Terms.
7. **Put both links in the footer of every page and form.** They must be easy to see and not hidden by pop-ups.

## Checkbox 1: Marketing texts (optional box)

Use this only if you send offers, promotions or news. Example for [MARKETING MESSAGE TYPES]: special offers, discounts and news.

> ☐ I agree to receive marketing text messages, such as [MARKETING MESSAGE TYPES], from [BUSINESS NAME] at the phone number I gave. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. See our [Privacy Policy]([PRIVACY POLICY URL]) and [Terms]([TERMS URL]).

## Checkbox 2: Non-marketing texts (optional box)

Use this for appointment reminders, service updates and answers to questions. Example for [NON-MARKETING MESSAGE TYPES]: appointment reminders, service updates and replies to my questions.

> ☐ I agree to receive non-marketing text messages, such as [NON-MARKETING MESSAGE TYPES], from [BUSINESS NAME] at the phone number I gave. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. See our [Privacy Policy]([PRIVACY POLICY URL]) and [Terms]([TERMS URL]).

## If you only send one kind of text

Keep just the one box that fits. A business that sends only reminders and updates keeps Checkbox 2. Say so in your campaign description.

## Form footer (every form and page)

> [Privacy Policy]([PRIVACY POLICY URL]) | [Terms of Service]([TERMS URL])

If you use a DBA, show it in the page header or footer too, for example: "[DBA NAME], a trade name of [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME]." Use the same name inside the checkbox text as in your registration.

## If your texts are age limited

If you sell age-limited products (for example alcohol or vape), add a real age check on the site and say "21 or older" (or the age your law requires) in the consent and Terms. Some categories are not allowed for business texting at all. Ask your attorney and read the carrier rules before you register.

## Sample confirmation message (sent right after someone joins)

Keep it under about 300 characters. It must name your business, say what they signed up for, say how often, mention rates, and show STOP and HELP.

> [BUSINESS NAME]: You are signed up for [MESSAGE TYPES] texts. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Terms: [TERMS URL]

## Sample STOP reply

> [BUSINESS NAME]: You have been unsubscribed and will get no more texts from us. Reply START to join again, or call [BUSINESS PHONE].

## Sample HELP reply

> [BUSINESS NAME]: For help, call [BUSINESS PHONE] or email [BUSINESS EMAIL]. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

## Sample campaign messages (use the same brand name)

Write one that is non-marketing and one that is marketing if you chose Low Volume Mixed. Use brackets for the parts that change.

> [BUSINESS NAME]: Hi [First Name], this is a reminder of your appointment on [Date] at [Time]. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

> [BUSINESS NAME]: Hi [First Name], this week only: [OFFER]. Book here: [LINK]. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

## Where else people might join

If people can also join by text keyword, paper form, QR code or in person, each way needs its own plain description in your campaign message flow, plus a public screenshot or link a reviewer can open without logging in.
