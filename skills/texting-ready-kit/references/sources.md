## Not confirmed

- The latest CTIA wording. Only the 2017 text could be read.
- Rules published directly by The Campaign Registry. HighLevel and Twilio apply them, so this kit follows their pages.
- Exact fee amounts. Read the current pricing in the account.
- A written rule that says "paid plan first." HighLevel says an active account is needed. Waiting for the paid plan is safe-practice advice.
- State laws. An attorney must check those.
